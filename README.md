# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY
Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to travel from one place to another with minimal human intervention. They use advanced technologies such as Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, GPS, cameras, radar, and LiDAR to understand their surroundings and make driving decisions.

Artificial Intelligence enables self-driving cars to recognize roads, detect pedestrians, identify traffic signals, avoid obstacles, and follow traffic rules. Machine Learning helps the vehicle learn from driving data, while Computer Vision analyzes images captured by cameras. Sensors measure the distance and speed of nearby objects, and GPS helps determine the vehicle's location.

AI algorithms process the collected information and decide when to accelerate, apply brakes, turn, or change lanes. These technologies help autonomous vehicles improve driving efficiency and reduce accidents caused by human error. However, self-driving cars still face challenges such as bad weather, unexpected obstacles, high costs, and safety concerns.

## WORKING PRINCIPLE

The working principle of a self-driving car involves the following steps:

1. Data Collection: Cameras, radar, LiDAR, ultrasonic sensors, and GPS collect information about the road, nearby vehicles, pedestrians, and the car's location.

2. Environment Perception: Computer Vision and AI algorithms analyze the collected data to identify lane markings, traffic lights, road signs, pedestrians, and obstacles.

3. Sensor Fusion: Data from different sensors are combined to obtain a more accurate understanding of the vehicle's surroundings.

4. Localization and Navigation: GPS and digital maps help the car determine its current position and identify the route to its destination.

5. Decision-Making: AI algorithms analyze road conditions and predict the movement of nearby objects. The system decides whether to accelerate, brake, turn, or change lanes.

6. Path Planning: The vehicle calculates a suitable and safe path while considering traffic conditions, road boundaries, and obstacles.

7. Vehicle Control: The control system sends commands to the steering, braking, and acceleration systems to move the vehicle along the planned path.

8. Continuous Monitoring: The vehicle continuously collects new sensor data and repeats these steps to respond to changing road conditions.
